Editorial Policy

Every reference guide on EMFLS Studio Journal is written so a practitioner can reproduce the geometry, check the underlying math, and verify the visual or acoustic outcome in their own workspace.

1. Measurement Transparency & Worked Examples

Worked tables and stop calculations across our Lighting, Design, and Studio sections illustrate optical, acoustic, or mathematical relationships under stated baseline assumptions—for instance, a fixed 1.20 m backdrop distance, a standard 6500 K viewing white point, or WCAG 2.x relative luminance formulas. Real studio outcomes always depend on wall paint reflectance, window orientation, lens transmission, display gamut, and room dimensions. We clearly label baseline assumptions and encourage readers to confirm target values with their own light meter, colorimeter, or reference test chart.

2. Editorial Independence & Advertising Separation

EMFLS Studio Journal is reader-first and maintains a strict firewall between editorial content and display advertising. We do not accept paid article placements, undisclosed sponsorships, paid link insertions, or free-hardware review exchanges. Whenever a camera format, color space, modifier shape, or file convention is named in a guide, it appears purely to illustrate a physical, optical, or workflow principle—never as a paid endorsement.

Advertising revenue from Google AdSense helps support ongoing research, writing, and hosting. Advertisers have zero influence over topic selection, conclusions, diagrams, or corrections.

3. Original Photography, Schematics & Accessibility

Each guide pairs high-resolution visual references with custom scalable vector schematics (`720 × 320` viewBox) accompanied by descriptive alternative text and readable captions. Every schematic includes a “Show full-size diagram” toggle and keyboard-scrollable viewport so mobile readers and assistive-technology users can inspect small angle markers and meter values without clipping or zooming glitches.

4. Fact-Checking, Updates & Substantive Revisions

Before publication, every article passes automated structural, HTML, link-integrity, schema, and reading-clarity checks in addition to editorial review of equations, units, and standards citations (including W3C WCAG specifications, IEC sRGB / Display P3 definitions, and standard photographic exposure formulas).

  • Substantive corrections or updates: When a formula, threshold, or technical standard changes, we update the text, recalculate affected tables, and refresh the guide's visible modification date.
  • Minor maintenance edits: Typographical fixes, spacing improvements, or broken-link repairs are deployed promptly without inflating publication dates.

5. Reader Corrections & Feedback Process

We welcome technical scrutiny from working photographers, designers, and engineers. If you spot a calculation slip, ambiguous diagram label, broken internal link, or accessibility barrier, email us directly at emfls24777@daum.net or open a public report on our Contact page. Include the article URL and the specific table row or schematic label; we review and respond to actionable technical reports within 2 to 4 business days.

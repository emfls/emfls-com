Editorial Policy

Our reference guides explain how to check a calculation or technique so readers can reproduce it in their own setup.

1. Measurement & Technical Accuracy

Worked examples illustrate calculations and measurement procedures. Results depend on the equipment, room, and settings used. Check the assumptions in each example before using its numbers as a target.

2. Independence & Conflict Prevention

EMFLS Studio Journal accepts no paid placement, undisclosed sponsorships, or commercial review exchanges. Product categories and tools mentioned in our lighting or studio setup notes serve purely as technical examples illustrating physics, optics, or software behavior.

3. Original Technical Diagrams

Article figures use scalable SVGs and text captions. Choose “Show full-size diagram” when labels are too small in the preview.

4. Corrections

See the Contact page to report a calculation error, unclear diagram label, or outdated reference. Include the article URL and the specific passage or label.

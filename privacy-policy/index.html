Privacy Policy

This policy explains how our host, advertising partners, and editorial email channel process data when you visit www.emfls.com.

1. Hosting & Static Site Search

GitHub Pages hosts this static site. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ Internet Protocol (IP) addresses for security and reliability, including visits by people who are signed out of GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Reading the site requires no account, login, or newsletter signup. On-site search downloads a static article index JSON file and filters your search query locally inside your browser without transmitting search terms to an external database.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

This site integrates Google AdSense to support independent publishing. When advertising services load, your browser shares standard technical information with Google, including the page URL and IP address. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies, web beacons, or similar identifiers to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet, measure ad performance, and help prevent fraud or invalid traffic. Read how Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services.

3. Your Advertising & Cookie Choices

  • Opt out of personalized advertising from Google via Google Ad Settings.
  • Opt out of third-party vendor personalized advertising cookies via YourAdChoices (aboutads.info).
  • Clear or block cookies at any time through your browser privacy controls.

4. Direct Email Correspondence

If you email the EMFLS Editorial Desk at emfls24777@daum.net, your email provider and ours process your address and message content solely to read and reply to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or add reader email addresses to marketing lists. Please do not include sensitive financial, medical, or account credentials in email correspondence.

5. Children's Information

EMFLS Studio Journal is a general technical reference resource intended for creative practitioners and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Privacy Requests & Policy Updates

For data handled by our host or advertising provider, see the GitHub Privacy Statement or Google Privacy Policy. For site-specific privacy questions or removal of email correspondence, write directly to emfls24777@daum.net or review the Contact page.

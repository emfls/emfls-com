Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to EMFLS Studio Journal. This document explains what limited technical data is collected when you visit www.emfls.com, how cookies and Google AdSense operate, and how you can manage or opt out of personalized advertising.

1. Log Files & Technical Requests

EMFLS Studio Journal is served as a static website. Like standard web hosting providers, our static hosting infrastructure may temporarily log non-identifying request headers such as browser type, referring URL, requested path, timestamp, and Internet Protocol (IP) address solely to maintain uptime, diagnose routing issues, and prevent abuse.

2. Cookies & Web Beacons

This website itself does not set account logins or proprietary tracking databases. Static client-side features such as in-page article search execute locally within your browser without uploading personal profiles.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

EMFLS Studio Journal participates in Google AdSense to support ongoing independent editorial documentation. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies—including the DoubleClick / Google advertising (DART) cookie—to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites across the Internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve advertisements to visitors based on their visit to www.emfls.com and other sites on the web.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can review how Google uses information from sites that use its services at Google Advertising Policies & Technologies.
  • Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Children's Information

EMFLS Studio Journal is a general technical reference resource intended for creative practitioners and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Consent & Contact

By using www.emfls.com, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you have privacy questions or requests regarding data handling, contact contact@emfls.com.

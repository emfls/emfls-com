Privacy Policy

This policy explains how our host and advertising providers process data when you visit www.emfls.com.

1. Hosting & Site Search

GitHub Pages hosts this static site. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ Internet Protocol (IP) addresses for security, including visits by people who are signed out of GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Reading the site requires no account. The site search downloads an article index and matches your search terms in your browser.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

This site loads Google AdSense. Your browser shares information with Google when its advertising services load, including the page URL and your IP address. Third parties, including Google, can place or read cookies in your browser and use web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers to collect information as a result of serving ads.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud or abuse. Depending on your settings, Google and its partners may use information about previous visits to this site and other sites to personalize ads. Read how Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services.

3. Advertising Choices

  • Manage Google ad personalization in Google Ad Settings.
  • Manage personalized advertising from participating vendors through YourAdChoices.

4. Children's Information

EMFLS Studio Journal is a general technical reference resource intended for creative practitioners and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Privacy Requests

For data handled by our host or ad provider, see the GitHub Privacy Statement or Google Privacy Policy. This site does not currently offer a private contact channel. Keep personal information out of the public technical issue tracker linked on the Contact page.

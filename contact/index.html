Reader Support & Corrections

Contact Us

Have a technical correction, accessibility report, diagram question, or privacy request? Contact the EMFLS Editorial Desk directly by email at emfls24777@daum.net or log a reproducible technical issue on GitHub.

Direct Editorial Email (Private Channel)

For editorial questions, privacy inquiries, copyright notices, or feedback you prefer to keep private, email emfls24777@daum.net. We review incoming messages regularly and aim to respond to actionable editorial and privacy inquiries within 2 to 4 business days.

Public Technical Correction Tracker

For open technical corrections—such as an equation check, broken link, table typo, or mobile layout defect—you can also open an issue in the public EMFLS Studio Journal GitHub issue tracker so fixes remain publicly auditable. Posting on GitHub requires a free GitHub account; existing reports remain publicly readable without signing in.

Helpful Details to Include

  • The full article URL on www.emfls.com.
  • The exact heading, table row, or diagram label you are referencing.
  • For layout or accessibility bugs, your browser name and approximate viewport width (for example, mobile 375 px or desktop 1440 px).

How Editorial Corrections Are Verified & Logged

When you flag a technical discrepancy—such as a lighting falloff calculation, contrast ratio, or CSS layout rule—our editorial desk reproduces the exact conditions on calibrated test fixtures or browser viewports before editing the source file. Confirmed fixes are rebuilt across the static site, validated against automated accessibility and broken-link checks, and reflected in the article’s updated revision date.

Please note that we do not accept paid guest posts, sponsored product placements, or paid link exchanges. Keep confidential passwords or sensitive personal documents out of both email and public GitHub reports. See our Privacy Policy and Editorial Policy for full details.
